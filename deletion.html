Data Safety & Deletion Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026 • Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Zero Cloud Data Storage: 0.1% Alarm does not require user accounts, emails, or logins. All your data (alarms, preferences, streak records) resides exclusively on your local device storage.

1. How Your Data is Stored

0.1% Alarm operates entirely offline. The app stores:

  • Your configured alarm times, repeat days, and labels.
  • Your selected challenge object list and quiz difficulty settings.
  • Your wake streak and wake completion records.

All of the above records are stored exclusively inside the application's private, encrypted sandboxed database on your phone.

2. How to Delete Your Data

Because we do not store any personal data on external cloud servers, you have full and immediate control over deleting your data at any time:

  • Deleting Individual Alarms: Swipe or tap "Delete alarm" within the app to immediately delete specific alarms.
  • Clearing All Data: Open Android Settings > Apps > 0.1% Alarm > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This instantaneously wipes all local databases, preferences, and wake records.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling 0.1% Alarm permanently and completely removes all application files and stored records from your device.

3. Account Deletion Requests

Since 0.1% Alarm does not create user accounts, there are no remote user accounts to deactivate or delete. If you have any questions or need verification, you can contact our developer support team at work.shivprasadrahulwad@gmail.com.
